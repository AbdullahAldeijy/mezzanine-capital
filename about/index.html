About Us

The Challenge

Enterprises need to prove the sustainability and readiness of their businesses, while funders need clearer data that helps them understand enterprises and assess risk.

The Solution

An ecosystem that brings together market and technical knowledge, data analysis, and financial and investment solutions in one connected path.

Who We Are

Mezzanine is a financial, investment and technology ecosystem that addresses the gap between the needs of small and medium enterprises and the requirements of funders, through the integration of data, credit analysis, and financial and investment solutions.

Vision

Leadership in building a smart and balanced commercial credit ecosystem.

Mission

Supporting enterprise sustainability and strengthening funders' confidence to drive development.
