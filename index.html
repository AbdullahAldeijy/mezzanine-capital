WHY THE GAP PERSISTS

A loop that keeps SMEs out of bank credit

  1. 1SMEs can't prove they're sustainable
  2. 2Funders can't measure the risk
  3. 3Cost of funds rises (COF)
  4. 4Cash cycle stretches (CCC)

And back to the start

Enterprises can't show institutional maturity, market resilience or solid capitalization. Funders can't lend efficiently, control the risk or flex policy for them. Each side's weakness raises the other's cost.

The core bottleneck is information asymmetry: evidence, context and control do not travel together.
